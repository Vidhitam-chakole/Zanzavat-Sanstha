Zanzavat Events • October 25, 2025

Festival Season Ration Distribution

The Story

Impact and Service Highlight

During the festive season of October 2025, Zanzavat launched a large-scale ration distribution program targeting daily wager households and roadside dwellers across multiple villages in the Nagpur district.

A total of 45 dry grocery kits were distributed, each containing flour, cooking oil, rice, lentils, and basic spices — enough to sustain a family for a full month. In addition, hot freshly cooked meals were served to over 200 roadside dwellers, ensuring no family went hungry during festival celebrations.

Feed Families in Need

A monthly ration kit costs very little but provides food security for an entire daily wage family.
