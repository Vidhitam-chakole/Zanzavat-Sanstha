Annual School Kit Distribution Drive

The Story

Impact and Service Highlight

Zanzavat Bahuudeshiya Shaikshanik Sanstha organized its flagship annual school kit distribution drive in June 2026. Volunteers traveled to rural blocks across the Nagpur district to deliver complete educational kits to children who otherwise could not afford basic school supplies.

Each kit contained a durable school bag, primary notebooks, compass boxes, pencil sets, and drawing kits. The event served over 75 children across 3 rural schools, ensuring they started the academic year with confidence, dignity, and the resources they need to succeed in their studies.
