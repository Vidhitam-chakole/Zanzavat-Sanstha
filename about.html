History

A Legacy of Hope & Commitment

Founded, Zanzavat Bahuudeshiya Shaikshanik Sanstha was established during a critical era of rural development in Maharashtra. The organization began as a small group of educators and social activists who wanted to solve basic resource deficits for students. Over the years, our initiatives expanded, growing from basic tuition groups to full-scale educational sponsorships, health checkup camps, clothing distribution drives, and community food projects.

Our foundational vision has always been to build a strong foundation, or आधार, for every child. We believe that by providing resources and guidance, we can help children break the cycle of poverty and lead a life of dignity, learning, and productivity.
