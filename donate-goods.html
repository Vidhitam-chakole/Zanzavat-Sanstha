Direct In-Kind Giving

Direct Impact Through Everyday Essentials

Not all contributions need to be financial. Many families and students in rural Maharashtra lack access to basic school supplies, warm winter clothes, or nutritious food staples. Zanzavat Sanstha collects, sorts, and delivers tangible goods directly into the hands of those in need with total accountability.

Simple 3-Step Donation Process

1
Submit Item Details

Fill out the form with the essentials you would like to contribute.

2
Coordination & Confirmation

Our volunteer coordinator connects via phone/WhatsApp within 24–48 hours to confirm logistics.

3
Community Handover

Items are sorted, verified, and delivered directly into the hands of underprivileged students and families.
