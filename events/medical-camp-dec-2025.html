Winter Medical Checkup Camp

The Story

Impact and Service Highlight

In December 2025, Zanzavat organized a comprehensive winter medical checkup camp in coordination with 8 volunteer general practitioners and pediatricians. The camp focused on children's wellness, basic eye testing, blood pressure monitoring, diabetes screening, and distribution of vitamins and primary medicines.

Over 120 patients were screened during the day-long camp, with critical cases identified and referred for free specialized medical treatment. Pediatric supplements and vitamin drops were distributed to children, while senior citizens received free eye checkups and reading glasses.
