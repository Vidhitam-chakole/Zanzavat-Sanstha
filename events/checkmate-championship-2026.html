Checkmate Championship Chess Tournament

The Story

Impact and Service Highlight

The Checkmate Championship is one of Vidarbha's largest annual chess tournaments organized by Zanzavat Bahuudeshiya Shaikshanik Sanstha. The 2026 edition brought together over 100 players from across the region, ranging from school students to adult enthusiasts.

The tournament promoted strategic thinking, sportsmanship, and excellence in the game of chess among youth. Prizes and certificates were distributed to winners and participants, with special recognition for young talent from rural communities. The event also served as a community engagement platform, strengthening bonds between urban and rural participants.
